    Facilities at TPS

    Smart Classrooms

    Bright, well-equipped classrooms that make learning interactive and engaging.

    Library

    A quiet space with books, journals and reading material for every age group.

    Playground

    A spacious ground for sports, games and physical activities.

    Computer Lab

    Computers for hands-on learning, so students build practical skills early.

    Science Lab

    Labs for Physics, Chemistry and Biology to learn by experiment.

    Transport

    School vehicles are available for safe travel to and from school.

    Learning Beyond the Classroom

    Since 1997, The Times Public School has grown from a humble and honest beginning into a pioneer in education in Udgir, with more than 900 students and 50+ teachers.

    We encourage children to choose the activities they love, and we celebrate every effort, from classroom success to sports, culture and community.
